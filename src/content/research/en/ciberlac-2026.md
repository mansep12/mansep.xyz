An international, in-person and intensive cybersecurity school, held from August 5 to 14, 2026, in Florianópolis, Brazil. It was organized by the Inter-American Development Bank (IDB) and Red CiberLAC, the Latin American and Caribbean network of excellence in cybersecurity.

### Courses

- **MITRE ATT&CK**: modeling adversary tactics and techniques.
- **Post-quantum cryptography (PQC) adoption**: criteria to prepare the migration.
- **Blockchain fundamentals**: technical foundations and use cases.
- **Information asset management** with free software.

### Practice

CTF Tectonic, a capture the flag competition. There were also workshops and talks on incident response, personal data protection in research, critical infrastructure in Latin America, digital forensics and social engineering.

What I most want to follow up on from the school is the migration to post-quantum cryptography and blockchain, because both are close to my thesis.

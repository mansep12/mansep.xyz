I'm Manuel Sepúlveda. I build backends and smart contracts where signatures, privacy and data provenance matter. I'm completing a double degree at Universidad de Chile, Computer Engineering and an M.Sc. in Computer Science, which closes with a single thesis.

My thesis, at CLCERT, designs a cryptographic mechanism to publish statistics of a verifiable electronic vote without compromising ballot secrecy. It is submitted and under review; I explain it in more detail on the [Research](/en/research) page.

From 2024 to 2026 I worked as a software developer at DIMACOFI. I built a multi-tenant kiosk for schools from scratch, which was installed in a school, and automated the update of more than 2,000 billing-critical addresses in Oracle EBS with Python and Selenium.

In October 2026, Nodus won Best University Project (1st place, University Track) at Find Your Way Hackathon (Tellus and Stellar). Nodus nets circular debts between SMEs in a single transaction, moving only the net balance. In August 2026 I was one of three CLCERT students selected to represent Universidad de Chile at the CiberLAC Cybersecurity School.
